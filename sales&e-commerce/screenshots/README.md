# Dashboard Screenshots & Visual Documentation

This directory is designated for visual documentation and dashboard screenshots of the **Sales & E-Commerce Performance Analytics Dashboard**.

## Captured Dashboard Views

The following views demonstrate the capabilities of the application:
1. `01_executive_overview.png`: High-level KPI Scorecard (Revenue, Profit, Margin, AOV, Orders) and core trend charts.
2. `02_sales_and_trends.png`: Monthly time-series performance, Month-over-Month (MoM) growth, and regional breakdown.
3. `03_profitability_and_discounts.png`: Category margin comparison and discount bracket erosion analysis.
4. `04_product_analytics.png`: Top 10 products by revenue and identification of bottom loss-making SKUs.
5. `05_customer_insights.png`: Segment sales share and client repeat purchase metrics.
6. `06_business_insights.png`: Dynamically calculated business findings and actionable recommendations.
7. `07_data_quality_audit.png`: Before-and-after data cleaning statistics, missing values, and IQR outlier boundaries.
8. `08_data_export_and_pdf.png`: Cleaned CSV/XLSX downloads and executive PDF reporting suite.
