# Executive Analytics Reports

This directory stores generated business intelligence and performance reports.

## Automated PDF Reporting Feature

The application provides on-demand PDF report generation implemented through Python's `reportlab` library:
- **Project Title & Metadata:** Scope, timestamp, total records audited.
- **Executive KPI Table:** Total Revenue, Net Profit, Profit Margin (%), Total Orders, Quantity Sold, Average Order Value (AOV), and Average Discount (%).
- **Measured Business Findings:** Dynamically calculated statistical observations (top regions, margin drivers, seasonal peak).
- **Strategic Recommendations:** Prioritized, data-backed action items for management.
- **Top Product & Category Breakdowns:** Revenue and margin tables.
- **Data Governance Summary:** Before-and-after cleaning statistics and duplicate/missing audit figures.

Reports can be downloaded directly from the **📥 Export & PDF Report** page in the Streamlit application.
